# DeltaForce AI V5 Knowledge Dataset

This project contains 382 validated curated knowledge records. The dataset is designed for grounded local RAG, not as a claim of complete coverage of every game statistic.

Main coverage:
- Official firearm catalog: weapon presence and weapon category, with Arabic and English retrieval records.
- Operations maps: Zero Dam, Layali Grove, Brakkesh, Space City, Tide Prison and AZ3.
- Official collectible/item names from the Delta Force HQ tool.
- Official weapon-attachment examples from the Delta Force Weapon Assembly event.
- Meltdown, Echo, Ahsarah, War Ablaze and Morphosis season/update facts.
- Current August 27, 2026 update information and the officially announced Reorientation launch date (September 8, 2026).
- Operators, weapons, ammunition, maps, vehicles, bosses, Operations systems and events.
- Operations tournament and RISE SERIES esports rules from the earlier curated project data.
- Recommendations/community/system-policy records remain separated from official facts.

Important design rule: a record confirms only what its source supports. Generic firearm catalog records confirm weapon presence/type and intentionally do not invent damage, recoil or other stats.
